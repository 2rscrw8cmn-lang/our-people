# Our People

A shared modern Rolodex for remembering the people in our lives.

Our People is intentionally small. It is not a CRM, task manager, social network, or relationship scorecard. The product centers on a deck of cards that helps a household remember people, keep small notes, and gently bring relationships back to mind without turning them into work.

## Current direction

- The deck is home.
- The card is the primary object.
- Search and Add are the only persistent controls.
- Relationship sections behave like dividers in the deck.
- Notes are quick jots, not records or tasks.
- Cards can represent one person, a couple, or a household.
- No AI, scores, dashboards, streaks, or productivity mechanics.

The first build target is a single vertical slice: open the deck, add people, open the new card, jot a note, and see that note on the card and in the notebook.
